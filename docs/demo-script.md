# Three-minute demonstration

Open the guided-demo URL from the README. No sign-in is required.

- 0:00–0:25: Explain Vishnu's problem: he is motivated but needs a role, a next task and clarity about submission.
- 0:25–0:55: Show the project plan and generate the idea brief.
- 0:55–1:25: Show the board, change a status and identify the next task's owner and completion condition.
- 1:25–1:55: Simulate one hour remaining. Review scope and show two optional tasks being cut.
- 1:55–2:25: Open pitch preparation and show the rehearsal timer.
- 2:25–2:50: Complete sample requirements and show readiness.
- 2:50–3:00: State the next validation step: test with a real team and an organiser.

The sample does not submit to an event, publish posts, connect external accounts or modify saved projects. A separate signed-in walkthrough can demonstrate creation, joining and saved progress; do not present that as verified with two real hosted accounts yet.
